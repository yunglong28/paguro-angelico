# Brief — Paguro angelico (Spring Draw mascot)

Distilled from the chat that produced the Spring Draw artifacts. Every character spec points here.

## What it is
The Spring Draw mascot: a hermit crab (paguro) living in a borrowed shell. Host and guest, the
residency draw: someone who lives in a house they did not build. Pushed toward the **esoteric /
medieval angel**: seraphim, ophanim, mandorlas, herbals, reliquaries. And Deleuze & Guattari:
*God is a lobster*, double articulation, two pincers.

## Rules (from the user's feedback, in order)
1. **3D, not a flat plate.** Real-time, rotatable.
2. **Real characters**, not abstract marks: eyes, a face, a body that acts.
3. **No crop marks, no captions, no useless text** on the image. The names are the only labels.
4. **More esoteric**: medieval angelology, not cute-only.
5. Not *only* a crab: the crab is the host; the angelic apparatus (wings, wheels, glory, crown,
   plant) is what makes each one a different character.

## Print grammar (Minor Index)
- Inks: **toner** (#141410) screened at 15°, **blu** (#001EF7) screened at 75° and slightly off
  register, **fluo** (#E8FF00) as a flat spot ink, used where medieval painting uses gold leaf
  (halos, crowns, rays, the drawn seed).
- Paper: cold #F4F4F2 with sparse blue fibres.
- Everything is shaded by coverage; there are no outlines.

## Lessons from the reference channel (`mascot.pdf`, Are.na "Mascot", 676 pp.)
- p.139: *"Low poly is ok, expression on faces and rigging is more important."* So every
  character shares one rigged eye with lids and six expressions.
- Olympic mascots (pp.307–320): mascots ship as **model sheets**: turnaround + expressions.
- Figures on white, plushes, keychains: the mascot is also an **object**, hence *Reliquiario*.
- p.252, crowned many-legged king: *Re Pescatore*.
- pp.256–257, herbal mandrakes: *Mandragora*.
- pp.442–444, nudibranchs and sea creatures: *Angelo di mare* (Clione, the "sea angel").

## Fixed elements (identity)
Blu body · toner spiral shell · stalk eyes · fluo seed held in the right claw (the draw).
