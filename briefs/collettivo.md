# Brief — Collettivo (Spring Draw, second series)

The same hermit crab, pushed from angelology toward **justice, solidarity and the commons**, by way
of weird Soviet-avant-garde references rather than propaganda iconography.

## Why the hermit crab already fits
- It lives in a house it did not build: housing as use, not ownership.
- **Vacancy chains** are real biology (Lewis & Rotjan 2009). When a bigger shell turns up, crabs
  queue by size and all move up one shell together. Redistribution without property: *Catena*.

## Rules
- Same as `paguro-angelico.md`: real characters, 3D, no captions, no text on banners or screens.
  The colour is the slogan.
- **Rosso** (#E3211A) joins as a second flat spot ink beside fluo, for this series only. In the
  print look it is a flat separation like fluo; in 3D it is a glossy red.
- Each character descends from an angel (see Genealogia): the crown becomes a tower, the seraph's
  voice becomes a megaphone, the ophan's wheels become a round dance.
