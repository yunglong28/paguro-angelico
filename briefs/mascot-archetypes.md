# Brief — Archetypes from the "Mascot" channel

Source: [are.na/trust-woznagislw0/mascot](https://www.are.na/trust-woznagislw0/mascot) (Trust 🌱 and 7 collaborators,
661 items) and its sub-channels `abstract-mascots` and `chara`, synced with `npm run refs -- <slug>` into
`briefs/references/<slug>/` (private, git-ignored; `index.json` keeps every block's title, source and comments).
Numbers below are block numbers in those `index.json` files.

Where the 90s board (`y2k-style.md`) is about *rendering* — gloss, chrome, box-art skies — this channel is a
cultural archive of the mascot itself: corporate and Olympic mascots, municipal characters, plush, folk demons,
internet creatures. It holds cute and unsettling together, as one comment on it puts it: *"darkness complimented
by cuteness"*. Seven families recur in it that the cast did not have; each is now an archetype in `engine/schema.js`.

| Archetype | What it is | References | New modules |
|---|---|---|---|

| **Sprout** | a head that grows a leaf | mascot #227 #428 #452 (Pikmin), #191 (TreeSense) | ears `sprout` |
| **Imp** | round and cute, with bat wings, horns, fangs and a devil tail | the comment above; chara (Gloomy Bear, Kuromi, devil mascots) | back `batwings`, tail `devil` |

The modules are not tied to their archetype: a costume mascot can grow gills, a sun can have a devil tail.
Archetypes are starting points; Randomize picks one and varies about half of its genes.

## Characters made from them (series *banca*)
Lumaca del baratto (sea slug), Pegno (plush), Mascotte di quartiere (costume), Ora solare (sun),
Occhio del registro (cyclops), Germoglio (sprout), Debito (imp). Each file lists its references.

## What not to take
About a third of the channel is screenshots of tweets and articles: context, not reference. Brand marks
(Olympic emblems, logos) are never copied; what is taken is construction — a cone and one lens, two rows of
gills, a head three times the body.
